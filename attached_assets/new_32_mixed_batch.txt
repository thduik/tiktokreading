Title: Sampling BiasTopic: Research Methods

Sampling bias occurs when the people or cases studied do not represent the wider group being discussed. A survey of online volunteers, for example, may miss people without internet access or those less motivated to respond. This does not make all surveys useless, but it means conclusions must be limited to what the sample can reasonably support. Careful sampling is therefore central to whether research claims travel beyond the group actually observed.

Vocab / Collocations:
